# Code of Conduct

**Attacking the claims is the work. Attacking people is not.**

That single line is the whole of it. Everything below is detail.

Nova-Violet Role builds tools that make **measurable** assertions — theorem
counts, exit codes, timings, "this gate has a negative control". We publish
those numbers so that anyone can falsify them. A community organised around
falsifiable claims only works if the claims are what get attacked.

---

## 1. What is always in bounds

- Proving a claim in our documentation is **false**. This is the most welcome
  contribution there is, and it has its own issue form.
- Showing that a test, gate or proof is **vacuous** — that it passes whether or
  not the property it names holds.
- Arguing that a design is wrong, that a benchmark is unrepresentative, or that
  a licence choice is a mistake.
- Saying "I could not reproduce this", and being taken seriously for it.
- Disagreeing with a maintainer, in public, at length, with evidence.

Nothing on that list is rudeness. A person who breaks our build and shows the
receipt has done us a service, and will be thanked for it in the changelog.

## 2. What is never in bounds

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained hostility toward a person.
- Slurs or demeaning language about anyone's identity — including but not
  limited to age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
  identity or expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status,
  nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity.
- Sexualised language, imagery, or attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately wasting contributors' time: knowingly false reports, fabricated
  measurements, or output pasted as if it had been run when it had not.

That last one belongs on this list and not in a style guide. **A fabricated
measurement is a conduct problem, not a technical one.** Our entire method rests
on numbers being real. Inventing one is the only way to genuinely poison this
community, and it is treated with the same seriousness as any other breach.

## 3. Honest failure is protected

You will never be penalised here for:

- not knowing something,
- not being able to verify a claim on your platform,
- opening a report that turns out to be your own configuration,
- writing "I could not check this" in a pull request.

**An honest gap is a result. A fabricated green is not.** If you are unsure,
say so plainly and ship the uncertainty with the work. That is the culture, and
it is enforced in your favour.

## 4. Scope

This applies in every space the organisation runs — issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit messages, code review, releases — and whenever someone is
representing Nova-Violet Role in public.

## 5. Enforcement

Report a breach privately to the organisation owner:

- open a **private security advisory** on the affected repository (this works
  for conduct reports too, and is the most private channel we have), or
- contact [@Saimonokuma](https://github.com/Saimonokuma) directly on GitHub.

Reports are read by the organisation owner. You will get an acknowledgement. If
your report concerns the owner, GitHub's own
[abuse reporting](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) is the correct route
and using it is not disloyal — it is the designed escalation path.

**Responses, in order of severity:**

| | Response |
|:--|:--|
| 1 | A private note naming the behaviour and what to do instead. |
| 2 | A public correction on the thread, and a warning. |
| 3 | A temporary block from interaction with the organisation's repositories. |
| 4 | A permanent ban. |

We will say which one we applied and why. An enforcement action that cannot be
explained is not one we will take.
