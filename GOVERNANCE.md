# Governance

Small, honest, and written down so nobody has to guess.

---

## Who decides

Nova-Violet Role is a non-profit organisation currently maintained by a single
owner, [@Saimonokuma](https://github.com/Saimonokuma), who holds final say on
every repository.

Pretending otherwise would be theatre. This document exists so that the
consequences of that structure are explicit rather than discovered.

## What that means for you

**You are not at the mercy of a mood.** The rules that decide whether your
contribution lands are written down and apply to the maintainer too:

- A claim that has not been measured does not ship — including one of ours.
- A gate that is red blocks the commit — including the maintainer's commit.
- A test nobody has watched fail is not evidence — including ours.

If a maintainer merges something that violates these, that is a defect, and
opening an issue about it is a legitimate contribution.

## How decisions get made

| Decision | How |
|:--|:--|
| Bug fix | Merge when the evidence is attached and gates are green. |
| New feature | Discuss in **Ideas** first if it changes the shape of the tool. |
| Breaking change | Announced in **Announcements**, with the reason and migration. |
| Licence change | Never done silently. Announced before it happens. |
| Something is wrong with our claims | Fixed with priority over new features. |

## Disagreement

Argue it in public, with evidence. If the maintainer says no, you will get a
reason — not silence, and not a locked thread. "No, and here is why" is the
minimum acceptable answer.

If you think that reason is wrong, you may say so once more, plainly. After
that, these are copyleft-licensed projects: **you can fork, and that is a
feature of the arrangement, not a failure of it.**

## If this organisation goes quiet

Everything here is licensed **AGPL-3.0-or-later OR EUPL-1.2**. If maintenance
stops, the code, the proofs, and the history remain yours to carry forward. That
is the entire reason for choosing copyleft rather than a permissive licence: the
work cannot be enclosed, by us or by anyone else.

## Adding maintainers

If this grows beyond one person, this document is updated **before** the second
maintainer is added, not after. Any change to it is announced.
