# Privacy Policy

Version 1, dated 2026-09-03. Applies to every repository in the Nova-Violet
Role organisation unless that repository ships its own `PRIVACY.md`, which
then takes precedence.

---

## Who is responsible

Nova-Violet Role is a non-profit organisation established in the European
Union, maintained by [@Saimonokuma](https://github.com/Saimonokuma), and the
controller for the little personal data it holds. Contact:
saimonokuma@gmail.com. A request about your data is answered within one
month, the period the GDPR sets.

## What we hold

Nothing beyond what you give GitHub in public and what Ko-fi shows a creator:

| data | where | how long | how to change or remove it |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| issue, discussion and pull-request text, with your GitHub handle | GitHub | while the repository exists | edit or delete your own post; ask us to delete a thread |
| commit metadata (name, email, date) of a contribution | the git history, in every clone and fork | permanent by design | a `.mailmap` entry corrects the name shown; we add one on request |
| a donation record | Ko-fi | per Ko-fi's policy | through Ko-fi |
| a security report | GitHub's private advisory | until published, then the advisory | you may ask to stay uncredited |

No telemetry, no analytics, no mailing list, no account, no server of ours.
Our software runs on your machine; a repository that sends anything anywhere
says so in its own `PRIVACY.md` or `SECURITY.md`, with the measurement.

## Other parties

GitHub processes what you post under its own privacy statement. Ko-fi
processes donations under its own. A tool of ours that runs inside Claude
Code sends your prompts to Anthropic through your own account, as every
Claude Code prompt does, and we receive nothing from that flow.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or
erased, object to a processing, or take it with you. Two limits are
structural: a commit in a public git history cannot be erased without
rewriting the history of every fork, so erasure there means a `.mailmap`
correction; and what you posted on GitHub is deleted through GitHub's own
controls. You may complain to the supervisory authority of your member state.

## Children

Our repositories are developer tooling and are not directed at children.

repository says otherwise. The licence governs what you may do with the work;
this policy describes what we do with data. Neither changes the other.
