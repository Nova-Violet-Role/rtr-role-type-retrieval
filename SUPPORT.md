# Support

There is no support contract here, and no queue. There is a small number of
people who care a lot about these tools being correct. Here is how to reach the
right one fastest.

---

## Pick the channel by what you have

| You have | Go to |
|:--|:--|
| **A question** — "how do I…", "is this supposed to…" | The repository's **Q&A** discussion |
| **A claim in our docs that is false** | Issues → *A claim in the docs is false* |
| **A reproducible defect** | Issues → *Something is broken* |
| **A security finding** | **Do not open an issue** — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) |
| **An idea or design argument** | The repository's **Ideas** discussion |
| **Something you built with this** | The repository's **Show and tell** discussion |
| **A conduct concern** | See [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) |

If you genuinely cannot tell which one applies, open a **Q&A discussion**. It
will be moved for you and nobody will mind.

## What makes a question answerable

You do not need to be polished. You do need to be reproducible:

- the exact command you ran, and its **exit code**,
- what you expected, and what actually happened,
- your OS, and the version of the tool (a commit hash is ideal),
- whether you have tried it on a clean checkout.

"It doesn't work" is not answerable and will only cost you a round trip.

## What to expect

This is a small non-profit organisation. Answers arrive when a maintainer is at
a keyboard, not on a schedule. What we commit to instead:

- **Questions are never treated as noise.** If our documentation had answered
  it, you would not be asking. An unanswerable question is usually our defect,
  and often becomes a documentation fix.
- **We say "I don't know" out loud** rather than guessing convincingly.
- **We will tell you if we are not going to do something**, instead of leaving
  the thread open forever.

## Paying for it

You never have to. If you want to,
[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/saimonokuma) exists — it buys time, not priority, and
support requests are answered in the same order regardless.
